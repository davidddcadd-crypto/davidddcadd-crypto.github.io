05Work, up close

Two of the trades behind this record, drawn out step by step — what the work involves, for anyone who has not done it. The drawings are redrawn illustrations of the trade, not records of a specific site or piece.

Process study A · Construction layout

From the drawing to a mark on the slab

What layout work involves: reading positions off the drawings, measuring them out on site and marking them, so that what gets built lands where the design says it should.

  1. 01Drawing

    Gridlines, dimensions and levels on the technical drawings fix where each column, wall and opening belongs.

  2. 02Measurement

    Surveying instruments — total station, laser, level — measure angles, distances and heights from known control points on site.

  3. 03Field notes

    Readings are written down point by point, with quick calculations to check them against the values on the drawing.

  4. 04Marking

    Gridlines are snapped onto the slab and points are marked, with offset lines kept where the true line will be covered.

  5. 05Built

    Formwork, rebar and columns are set out from those marks, so everything built afterwards depends on their accuracy.

NoteRedrawn illustration of the trade, not to scale — not a record of a specific site, drawing or project.

Process study B · Jewellery purchasing & quality control

From a design reference to a purchasing decision

What purchasing and quality-control work in the jewellery trade involves: checking stones and finished pieces against what was specified, then weighing quality against cost.

  1. 01Reference

    A design reference or sketch sets out the piece: its form, its metal and the stones it calls for.

  2. 02Inspection

    Loose stones and finished pieces are examined under magnification for cut, finish and visible characteristics.

  3. 03Grading documentation

    Laboratory grading reports are read alongside the stone: shape, measurements, proportions and the grades stated.

  4. 04Stone evaluation

    Stones are compared side by side on colour and cut, and diamonds are checked for CVD lab-grown material before purchase.

  5. 05Purchasing judgment

    Supplier quotations, costs and materials are compared to weigh quality against price, and the order is followed through to shipment.

NoteRedrawn illustration of the trade — not a record of a specific client item, stone, supplier or report. Document details are deliberately left out.
